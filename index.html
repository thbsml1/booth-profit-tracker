Built for booth vendors, not accountants

If you rent space in an antique mall, flea market, vintage co-op or consignment shop, you pay rent every month whether things sell or not. Then the store takes its cut.

Most vendors keep it on paper or in their head and find out months later the booth was losing money. This tracker shows you every month, in plain numbers.

  • Your break-even numberHow much you need to sell each month (and each week) before the booth makes a dime.
  • Rent per square footWhat every foot of your booth has to earn just to pay for itself.
  • Profit on every itemAfter commission, card fees and what you paid for it. Figured automatically.
  • Stale stock turns redAnything sitting too long gets flagged so you know what to mark down or pull.
  • Monthly profit or lossTwelve months side by side, with a plain "made money" or "lost money".

Set up in about 10 minutes

  1. Type in your rent, the store's commission and your booth size.

  2. Log items as they go into your booth, and the sale price when they sell.

  3. Open the Monthly Summary to see if the booth paid for itself.
